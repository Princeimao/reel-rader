# ReelRadar: will this Instagram Reel work?
ReelRadar is an Instagram Reels trend analyst. Ask whether a reel idea will work, or what is trending in your niche, and it researches the live web, then returns a verdict with a score, the reasons, cited sources, concrete improvements and three hook options. Send a video, a screenshot or a public YouTube link and it also watches the content and critiques the hook, pacing, captions and audio.

## What it can do

- Judge a reel idea: score out of 100, verdict (likely to work, could work with changes, unlikely to work) and confidence level
- Research current Instagram Reels trends, formats, hooks and ranking signals for any niche, with source links
- Watch an uploaded video or a public YouTube/Shorts link and give feedback on the first 3 seconds, pacing, on-screen text, audio and retention risks
- Review a cover or screenshot image
- Suggest improvements and 3 alternative hooks

## Example questions

- Will a "day in my life as a nurse" reel work right now?
- What Instagram Reels formats are trending for home workouts?
- Is a 15-second "3 mistakes beginners make in guitar" reel a good idea?
- Which hooks are working for food reels this month?
- Here is my video, will it perform? (attach the file)
- Review this reel: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/example
- What is the best format for a small bakery's reels?

## How it works

1. ASI:One's reasoning model reads the question and plans the research.
2. A live web search collects current articles about Reels trends and the algorithm.
3. If a video or image is provided, a video model watches it and describes only what it sees and hears.
4. The reasoning model combines both and writes the verdict. Every trend claim cites a numbered source.

## Limits (please read)

- Scores are estimates from public web research and your input. They are not Instagram analytics and nothing is guaranteed.
- It cannot open Instagram links, because Instagram blocks automated access. Upload the file, or share a public YouTube link.
- Uploaded files should be under about 14 MB.
- If live research is unavailable, the answer says so and confidence is lowered.
- Free agent with a limit of 12 requests per hour per user.
